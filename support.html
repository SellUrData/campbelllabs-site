Hopps tips

  • Place the phone on the floor 2–3 m (7–10 ft) away, with your whole body in frame.
  • Stand still for a moment until Hopps shows Ready, then jump.
  • For the most accurate results, record slow-motion video (120 or 240 fps) and analyze it in the app.
  • Your data is stored on your device. Use Settings → Backup before switching phones.
