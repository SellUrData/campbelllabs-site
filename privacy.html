Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

This policy explains how Campbell Labs LLC ("we", "us") handles information in the Hopps mobile app and on this website.

Camera and video

Hopps uses your device's camera, or videos you choose from your photo library, only to detect body position and measure jump height. All video is processed on your device. We do not record, upload, store or transmit your camera feed or videos.

Data you create

Jump results, goals and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that collect this data, and we cannot access it. You can export or delete it at any time in the app's Settings. Deleting the app removes it.

Information we collect

Hopps does not require an account. It does not collect personal information, and it contains no advertising or third-party tracking. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to respond to you.

Children

Hopps is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.
